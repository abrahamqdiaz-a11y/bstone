## Cannibalization guardrails

- **Estate cleanouts vs. inherited home cleanouts:** different H1s, titles and leads ("belongings after a death" vs. "a house you now own"). Each links to the other with explicit "if you need X instead" context. In Search Console, watch for both URLs ranking for the same query. If they swap positions persistently after 3–4 months, merge inherited-home into estate-cleanouts with a 301.
- **Estate transitions** is the umbrella/project offer. It targets "transition/preparation" phrasing, not "cleanout".
- **Home cleanouts** targets non-bereavement intent ("whole house cleanout", "rental property cleanout") and links out to estate pages for bereavement intent.
- Resources target "how to / checklist" queries and link to exactly one primary service each in their CTA block.
